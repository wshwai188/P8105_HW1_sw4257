## Problem 1

``` r
data("penguins", package = "palmerpenguins")
```

The `penguins` dataset contains measurements for 3 penguin species, and
they are Adelie, Chinstrap, Gentoo. The penguins were observed on
Biscoe, Dream, Torgersen islands. Important variables include `species`,
`island`, bill length and bill depth in millimeters, flipper length in
millimeters, body mass in grams, sex, and year. The dataset contains 344
observations and 8 variables. The mean flipper length is 200.92 mm.
